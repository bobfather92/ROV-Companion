RŌV Companion

A modern Android app for controlling the Rhino RŌV Pro Everyday camera slider, created as an alternative to the original RŌV Motion app.

**Version 1.0.0 · Requires Android 12 or later and Bluetooth LE.**

## Features

- Automatically finds and connects to your slider.
- Controls movement in both directions.
- Adjustable speed and ease in/out.
- Custom A and B travel positions from 0–100%.
- Loops between your selected positions.
- Keeps moving when switching apps or locking the screen.
- Stop and Disconnect controls in the notification.
- Saves your preferred settings.

## Getting started

1. Install the APK on your Android phone.
2. Close the original RŌV Motion app and switch on the slider.
3. Enable Bluetooth and Location, then open Companion.
4. Allow Nearby devices, precise Location while using the app, and notifications when prompted.
5. Once connected, choose your travel range, direction, speed and easing. Enable Loop if wanted, then press Start.

Use Stop in the app or notification to finish a move.

## Notes

The default travel range is 5–95%. Selecting 0–100% uses the physical calibrated endpoints. A and B must be at least 10 percentage points apart.

The app works offline without accounts or analytics. GPS coordinates are not collected.

Time-lapse and camera shutter controls are not added and will be included in a future releae. Other slider models are unverified. Version 1.0.0 has passed software and emulator checks; its latest background and full-travel features still need physical-device confirmation.
